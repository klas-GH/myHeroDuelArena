MY HERO DUEL ARENA — QUICK GUIDE

1. OPEN index.html IN A BROWSER. NO INSTALL, NO BUILD.
2. PICK A BATTLEFIELD AND A DIFFICULTY. THE CHOOSER LIVES IN THE LAST ROW
   AND OPENS THROUGH THE "BATTLEFIELDS" BUTTON, SO THE WHOLE TOP OF THE
   SCREEN BELONGS TO THE FIELD. CLICK A CARD TO REDRAW THE GROUND.
3. PRESS "ENTER THE ARENA" TO DEPLOY THE LEGIONS.
4. CLICK A BLUE UNIT TO SELECT IT.
   - BLUE CELLS SHOW WHERE IT CAN MOVE.
   - RED CELLS SHOW WHAT IT CAN STRIKE.
5. CLICK A BLUE CELL TO MOVE, A RED UNIT TO ATTACK.
   - A FIGHTER MAY MOVE OR ATTACK IN A TURN, NOT BOTH. INSTEAD OF EITHER, IT
     MAY BRACE BEHIND A SHIELD (THE DEFENCE STAND): IT GIVES UP ITS ACTION AND
     ITS ARMOUR RISES BY TWENTY POINTS UNTIL IT ACTS AGAIN.
   - A FIGHTER STRUCK BY A BLADE ANSWERS WITH A BLADE OF ITS OWN AT ONCE
     (THE RIPOSTE). A SHOOTER IS ANSWERED WITH STEEL, NOT WITH AN ARROW.
6. PRESS "END TURN" WHEN YOU ARE DONE. THE CRIMSON LEGION ANSWERS.
   - THE LEAD ALTERNATES: YOU OPEN THE FIRST ROUND, THE CRIMSON LEGION OPENS
     THE SECOND, YOU OPEN THE THIRD, AND SO ON. EVERY ROUND IS ONE MOVE FOR
     EACH SIDE, WHOEVER OPENED IT.
   - EVERY HIT PAINTS THE RECEIVER WITH A RED GLOW AND A ONE-SECOND SHUDDER,
     AND THE DEVICE VIBRATES FOR A SECOND WHEN IT CAN.
7. A BATTLE IS THE FIRST MATCH OF A DUEL. THE LEGION THAT TAKES TWO OF THREE
   MATCHES WINS THE DUEL, AND THE SERIES STOPS AS SOON AS ONE SIDE HAS TWO.
   THE AZURE LEGION OPENS THE FIRST MATCH, THE CRIMSON LEGION OPENS THE
   SECOND, AND THE DECIDER IS OPENED BY WHICHEVER SIDE THE DICE PICK.
   BETWEEN MATCHES PRESS "NEXT MATCH" TO FIGHT THE NEXT ONE (OR WAIT FOR THE
   SHOW TO ADVANCE ITSELF). THE VICTORY BONUS, THE EXPERIENCE AND THE
   WIN-LOSS RECORD BELONG TO THE DUEL, NOT TO THE MATCH THAT JUST ENDED.
8. WHEN THE DUEL ENDS, THE REPORT SHOWS WHAT IT WAS WORTH: ROUNDS, DAMAGE,
   DEFEATS, LOSSES AND THE EXPERIENCE YOU WERE PAID.

THE ARMOUR
- EVERY TROOP CARRIES ITS OWN ARMOUR: A SHARE OF EVERY INCOMING BLOW THAT
  TURNS AWAY BEFORE THE FIGHTER FEELS IT. ARMOUR MAKES A FIGHTER HARDER TO
  BRING DOWN, BUT NEVER A WALL — AN ARMOURED CHAMPION STILL FALLS IN A
  HANDFUL OF BLOWS.

THE FOUR BATTLEFIELDS
- THE OLD FRONTIER: OPEN GROUND AND TWO RISES. THE EVEN FIGHT.
- THE FOREST MARCH: DEEP WOOD ON BOTH FLANKS. COVER FOR WHOEVER HOLDS IT.
- THE BROKEN PASS: A FALLEN WALL SPLITS THE FIELD. FIND A WAY THROUGH.
- THE HIGH GROUND: A RAISED PLAATEAU IN THE MIDDLE. WHOEVER HOLDS IT
  SHOOTS FREE.
- EVERY MAP IS MIRRORED, SO NEITHER LEGION GETS THE BETTER GROUND, AND EVERY
  MAP LETS A FIGHTER WITH ONE MOVEMENT POINT REACH THE OTHER SIDE.

DIFFICULTY
- EASY: THE CRIMSON LEGION HESITATES, MISREADS THE FIELD AND HITS SOFTER.
- NORMAL: IT FIGHTS THE WAY IT ALWAYS HAS.
- HARD: IT READS THE FIELD, HUNTS YOUR CHAMPION, PRESSES HARD AND HITS
  HARDER. ITS BOWS LAND TWO POINTS STRONGER.
- THE DIFFICULTY ONLY CHANGES THE CRIMSON LEGION. YOUR OWN FIGHTERS ALWAYS
  FIGHT BY THE SAME RULES.

THE FIELD
- 10 COLUMNS AND 4 LANES. BLUE DEPLOYS IN COLUMNS 2 AND 3, RED IN COLUMNS 6
  AND 7, AND THE COLUMNS BETWEEN THEM ARE NEUTRAL GROUND.
- DISTANCE IS COUNTED THE OCTAGONAL WAY: THE LONGEST AXIS DECIDES. A CELL
  ONE COLUMN AND ONE ROW AWAY IS ONE STEP AWAY. A DIAGONAL STEP COSTS THE
  SAME AS A STRAIGHT ONE.
- A DIAGONAL STEP IS REFUSED WHEN BOTH CELLS IT WOULD SLIP BETWEEN ARE
  BLOCKED, BY A ROCK FORMATION OR BY ANOTHER FIGHTER.

TERRAIN
- GRASS: PLAIN GROUND.
- FOREST: +2 DEF, AND IT BLOCKS SIGHT. FLANK OR GO THROUGH IT.
- ROCKY GROUND: COSTS 2 TO CROSS, +1 DEF.
- HIGH GROUND: +1 DAMAGE FOR EVERY SHOT TAKEN FROM THERE.
- ROCK FORMATIONS: IMPASSABLE, AND THEY BLOCK SIGHT.

THE LEGION - 7 FIGHTERS
- FRONT RANK: 2 FOOT SOLDIERS AND 1 CHEVALIER, ONE COLUMN CLOSER TO THE ENEMY.
- CHAMPION'S COLUMN: 2 ARCHERS, 1 BALLISTA AND THE CHAMPION, ONE IN EVERY LANE.
- THE CHAMPION IS DRAWN ACROSS TWO COLUMNS, BUT IT STANDS ON ITS OWN CELL.

THE WEAPONS
- A BOW COVERS THE WHOLE FIELD. IT HITS ANY LIVING ENEMY ON THE FIELD, AND
  NEITHER THE DISTANCE, NOR THE GROUND IN BETWEEN, NOR THE RANK THE ENEMY
  STANDS IN, NOR THE RANK IN FRONT OF IT TURNS THE SHOT ASIDE.
- A BLADE ONLY CUTS FORWARD, IT REACHES TWO CELLS AND NEVER CUTS BACKWARDS.
  THE RANK IN FRONT OF THE TARGET ONLY TURNS THE BLOW ASIDE WHEN IT CLOSES
  EVERY LANE BETWEEN THE TWO FIGHTERS. ONE OPEN LANE IS A WAY IN, SO A GAP IN
  A LINE IS A REAL OPENING.
- HIGH GROUND PAYS ITS AIM BONUS TO A SHOT ONLY, NEVER TO A BLOW.

THE CHAMPION
- SELECT THE CHAMPION TO OPEN THE CHAMPION PANEL ON THE RIGHT.
- THE CHAMPION CARRIES TWO WEAPONS: ITS OWN BLOW AT ARM'S LENGTH (ATK 20)
  AND A BOW THAT COVERS THE FIELD (ATK 16). THE ARROW HITS SOFTER, BUT
  IT LETS THE CHAMPION FIGHT FROM THE RANKS IT GUARDS.
- READY ABILITIES APPEAR AS QUICK ACTIONS UNDER THE SELECTION PANEL.
  RALLY ALLIES  - LEVEL 1 - +3 ATK / +2 DEF TO ADJACENT ALLIES, ONE ROUND.
  POWER STRIKE - LEVEL 2 - 8 EXTRA DAMAGE, COSTS THE ATTACK.
  GUARDING AURA- LEVEL 3 - +3 DEF TO THE CHAMPION AND ITS ADJACENT ALLIES,
                 COSTS THE WHOLE TURN.
- EACH ABILITY COSTS SOMETHING DIFFERENT: THE ABILITY ALLOWANCE, THE
  ATTACK, OR THE WHOLE TURN. WATCH THE HINTS UNDER THE SELECTION PANEL.

PROGRESSION
- THE PROGRESSION STRIP UNDER THE TURN BANNER SHOWS LEVEL, XP AND POINTS.
- YOU EARN 1 XP FOR EVERY 5 DAMAGE YOU ACTUALLY LAND, PLUS XP FOR DEFEATS
  AND VICTORIES. DAMAGE THAT OVERSHOOTS A DYING TARGET IS NOT PAID FOR.
  EVERY LEVEL GRANTS AN ABILITY POINT AND A PERMANENT STAT BONUS. THE
  PROFILE IS SAVED IN THIS BROWSER.
- "RESET" IN THE STRIP CLEARS THE PROFILE. IT ASKS TWICE ON PURPOSE.

KEYS
- ENTER OR SPACE STARTS A NEW BATTLE.
- CLICK A UNIT TO SELECT IT, CLICK AGAIN ON A TARGET TO ACT.
