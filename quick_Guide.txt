MY HERO DUEL ARENA — QUICK GUIDE

1. OPEN index.html IN A BROWSER. NO INSTALL, NO BUILD.
2. PICK A BATTLEFIELD AND A DIFFICULTY IN THE PANEL ABOVE THE FIELD.
   EACH CARD SHOWS A PICTURE OF ITS OWN GROUND. CLICK ONE TO REDRAW THE
   FIELD. THE "HIDE" BUTTON FOLDS THE PANEL AWAY.
3. PRESS "ENTER THE ARENA" TO DEPLOY THE LEGIONS.
4. CLICK A BLUE UNIT TO SELECT IT.
   - BLUE CELLS SHOW WHERE IT CAN MOVE.
   - RED CELLS SHOW WHAT IT CAN STRIKE.
5. CLICK A BLUE CELL TO MOVE, A RED UNIT TO ATTACK.
6. PRESS "END TURN" WHEN YOU ARE DONE. THE CRIMSON LEGION ANSWERS.
7. WHEN THE BATTLE ENDS, THE REPORT SHOWS WHAT IT WAS WORTH: ROUNDS,
   DAMAGE, DEFEATS, LOSSES AND THE EXPERIENCE YOU WERE PAID.

THE FOUR BATTLEFIELDS
- THE OLD FRONTIER: OPEN GROUND AND TWO RISES. THE EVEN FIGHT.
- THE FOREST MARCH: DEEP WOOD ON BOTH FLANKS. COVER FOR WHOEVER HOLDS IT.
- THE BROKEN PASS: A FALLEN WALL SPLITS THE FIELD. FIND A WAY THROUGH.
- THE HIGH GROUND: A RAISED PLAATEAU IN THE MIDDLE. WHOEVER HOLDS IT
  SHOOTS FREE.
- EVERY MAP IS MIRRORED, SO NEITHER LEGION GETS THE BETTER GROUND, AND EVERY
  MAP LETS A FIGHTER WITH ONE MOVEMENT POINT REACH THE OTHER SIDE.

DIFFICULTY
- EASY: THE CRIMSON LEGION HESITATES, MISREADS THE FIELD AND HITS SOFTER.
- NORMAL: IT FIGHTS THE WAY IT ALWAYS HAS.
- HARD: IT READS THE FIELD, HUNTS YOUR CHAMPION, PRESSES HARD AND HITS
  HARDER. ITS BOWS LAND TWO POINTS STRONGER.
- THE DIFFICULTY ONLY CHANGES THE CRIMSON LEGION. YOUR OWN FIGHTERS ALWAYS
  FIGHT BY THE SAME RULES.

THE FIELD
- 10 COLUMNS AND 4 LANES. BLUE HOLDS THE LEFT THREE COLUMNS, RED THE RIGHT
  THREE, AND THE FOUR COLUMNS IN BETWEEN ARE NEUTRAL GROUND.
- DISTANCE IS COUNTED THE OCTAGONAL WAY: THE LONGEST AXIS DECIDES. A CELL
  ONE COLUMN AND ONE ROW AWAY IS ONE STEP AWAY. A DIAGONAL STEP COSTS THE
  SAME AS A STRAIGHT ONE.
- A DIAGONAL STEP IS REFUSED WHEN BOTH CELLS IT WOULD SLIP BETWEEN ARE
  BLOCKED, BY A ROCK FORMATION OR BY ANOTHER FIGHTER.

TERRAIN
- GRASS: PLAIN GROUND.
- FOREST: +2 DEF, AND IT BLOCKS RANGED FIRE. FLANK OR GO THROUGH IT.
- ROCKY GROUND: COSTS 2 TO CROSS, +1 DEF.
- HIGH GROUND: +1 DAMAGE FOR EVERY SHOT TAKEN FROM THERE.
- ROCK FORMATIONS: IMPASSABLE, AND THEY BLOCK SIGHT.

THE LEGION - 9 FIGHTERS, 2 OF EVERY KIND BUT THE CHAMPION
- FRONT RANK: 2 FOOT SOLDIERS AND 2 CHEVALIERS, ONE IN EVERY LANE.
- MIDDLE COLUMN: 2 BALLISTA AND 2 ARCHERS, ONE IN EVERY LANE.
- REAR GUARD: THE CHAMPION ALONE.

THE CHAMPION
- SELECT THE CHAMPION TO OPEN THE CHAMPION PANEL ON THE RIGHT.
- THE CHAMPION CARRIES TWO WEAPONS: ITS OWN BLOW AT ARM'S LENGTH (ATK 20)
  AND A BOW THAT REACHES THREE CELLS (ATK 16). THE ARROW HITS SOFTER, BUT
  IT LETS THE CHAMPION FIGHT FROM THE RANKS IT GUARDS.
- READY ABILITIES APPEAR AS QUICK ACTIONS UNDER THE SELECTION PANEL.
  RALLY ALLIES  - LEVEL 1 - +3 ATK / +2 DEF TO ADJACENT ALLIES, ONE ROUND.
  POWER STRIKE - LEVEL 2 - 8 EXTRA DAMAGE, COSTS THE ATTACK.
  GUARDING AURA- LEVEL 3 - +3 DEF TO THE CHAMPION AND ITS ADJACENT ALLIES,
                 COSTS THE WHOLE TURN.
- EACH ABILITY COSTS SOMETHING DIFFERENT: THE ABILITY ALLOWANCE, THE
  ATTACK, OR THE WHOLE TURN. WATCH THE HINTS UNDER THE SELECTION PANEL.

PROGRESSION
- THE PROGRESSION STRIP UNDER THE TURN BANNER SHOWS LEVEL, XP AND POINTS.
- YOU EARN 1 XP FOR EVERY 5 DAMAGE YOU ACTUALLY LAND, PLUS XP FOR DEFEATS
  AND VICTORIES. DAMAGE THAT OVERSHOOTS A DYING TARGET IS NOT PAID FOR.
  EVERY LEVEL GRANTS AN ABILITY POINT AND A PERMANENT STAT BONUS. THE
  PROFILE IS SAVED IN THIS BROWSER.
- "RESET" IN THE STRIP CLEARS THE PROFILE. IT ASKS TWICE ON PURPOSE.

KEYS
- ENTER OR SPACE STARTS A NEW BATTLE.
- CLICK A UNIT TO SELECT IT, CLICK AGAIN ON A TARGET TO ACT.
